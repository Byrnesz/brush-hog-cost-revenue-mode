⚠️ Risk Rules Explained

🚜 Mobilization Fee

Flat show-up fee that scales with distance:

  • 5 miles: $175
  • 10 miles: $200
  • 15 miles: $225
  • 20+ miles: $250

Covers transit time and fuel costs for distant jobs

🪨 Obstacle Multiplier

Adjusts revenue based on terrain difficulty:

  • Open Field: 1.0x (no adjustment)
  • Slopes: 1.2x (+20%)
  • Thick Brush: 1.5x (+50%)
  • Rocks/Heavy Debris: 1.8x (+80%)

Compensates for increased wear and slower progress

⏳ Down-Time Clause

Billing continues during delays when enabled:

  • Clearing barbed wire
  • Replacing shattered shear pins
  • Removing large obstacles
  • Equipment repairs

Set to 1 to enable, 0 to disable

⏱️ Minimum Charge

2-hour minimum for jobs under 2 acres:

  • Prevents losses on small lots
  • Covers setup and breakdown time
  • Auto-applies for < 2 acre jobs

Adjustable in the spreadsheet
